Wax epilation extracts hair from the root using warm or cold wax formulations. Modern hard-wax systems are engineered to grip the hair shaft while releasing cleanly from the skin, making them the preferred choice for sensitive zones such as the bikini line and underarms.

Results typically last three to four weeks. Consistent treatment often leads to progressively finer regrowth. Proper preparation (hair length of ¼–½ inch) remains essential for effective adhesion and minimal discomfort.
